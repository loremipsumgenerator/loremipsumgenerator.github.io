What Is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is the placeholder text designers have used for centuries. Learn what it means, where it comes from, and why it still matters.

A definition in plain language

Lorem ipsum is dummy text used to fill a design before the real words are ready. It looks like Latin, but it is mostly scrambled Latin with a few real words mixed in. Its job is to look like a normal paragraph, with realistic word lengths and sentence rhythms, without distracting the reader with meaning. Designers paste it into layouts so they can judge spacing, typography and balance before the copy arrives.

Where you meet it

You meet lorem ipsum in website mockups, app screens, print layouts, presentation templates and word-processor samples. It appears wherever a designer needs to show how a page will look when it is full of text. It is also the default filler in many design tools: type a shortcut in a prototyping app and a paragraph of lorem ipsum appears. Its ubiquity is its strength; everyone in design recognizes it instantly as a stand-in.

Why not use real words?

Real words distract. When a client reads a mockup filled with real copy, they start editing the words instead of judging the layout, and the meeting drifts into copywriting. Placeholder text keeps the focus on design: spacing, hierarchy, line length and balance. It also avoids the awkwardness of draft copy being mistaken for finished copy and published by accident.

Why not use random letters?

Random letters do not look like text. Real language has a rhythm of short and long words, commas and full stops, and the eye expects that pattern. Lorem ipsum mimics the word-length distribution of Latin, which is close enough to English to feel realistic at a glance. That realism is what makes a layout test meaningful: if the design works with lorem ipsum, it will usually work with real copy of similar length.

When to stop using it

Lorem ipsum is a stand-in, not a substitute. Replace it with real content as soon as the design is approved, and never ship a live page that still contains it. Real content changes everything: headings get longer, paragraphs get shorter, and the layout needs adjusting. Treat lorem ipsum as scaffolding: essential while building, embarrassing if left in the finished product.

The standard passage, word for word

The standard lorem ipsum passage begins 'Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua.' Most generators produce this opening and then continue with further sentences from the same tradition, such as 'Ut enim ad minim veniam, quis nostrud exercitation ullamco laboris nisi ut aliquip ex ea commodo consequat.' You do not need to memorize it; every generator on this site produces it for you. Knowing the opening is useful mainly for recognition: when you see those words, you know instantly that the text is a placeholder and that the design is not yet finished.

Frequently asked questions

Does lorem ipsum mean anything?

Mostly no. It is scrambled Latin, though the opening words come from a real sentence by the Roman writer Cicero. Individual words are real Latin, but the sentences have no coherent meaning.

Is lorem ipsum free to use?

Yes. It is in the public domain and has been used freely for centuries. You can paste it into any project without permission or payment.

Why is it always the same opening?

Tradition. The standard passage has opened with 'Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet' since the 1500s, so designers recognize it immediately.

How long is the standard passage?

The classic passage is about 180 words in its common form, though versions vary. Generators can produce any length you need.
