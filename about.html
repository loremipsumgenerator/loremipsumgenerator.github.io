About

LoremIpsumGenerator is a free utility site. Every tool runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type, pick or generate is uploaded to a server. We built it for designers, developers, students and anyone who needs a quick, reliable answer without signing up or installing software.

Each tool does one job well and shows its working so you can check the result yourself. The guides go deeper, with practical advice you can apply to real projects the same day.

We keep the site fast and lightweight: no tracking scripts, no external fonts, no ads. If you spot a mistake or have an idea for a new tool, email us at contact@loremipsumgenerator.github.io.
