Lorem Ipsum Alternatives

Bored of the same Latin? Explore readable English filler, themed generators, and real-content approaches that work better.

Readable English filler

The most practical alternative is filler text written in plain English, such as 'This is a sample paragraph that shows how your text will look in this space.' It reads naturally, so clients understand it is a stand-in, and it tests line length with real English word lengths. Some generators produce English-like sentences with realistic structure. The trade-off is that clients may start editing the filler as if it were real copy, so label it clearly as a draft.

Themed and funny generators

Dozens of themed generators exist: pirate lorem ipsum, zombie lorem ipsum, coffee lorem ipsum, and many more. They replace the Latin words with themed vocabulary while keeping the sentence structure. They are fun for internal mockups and for teams with a sense of humour, but they are risky in client work, where a joke can look unprofessional. Save them for internal drafts and use the classic text for anything a client will see.

Using your own draft copy

The best alternative is often real draft copy, even if it is rough. A rough paragraph written by the client or the copywriter tests the design with realistic lengths and reveals content problems early. It also keeps the project moving, because the draft becomes the final copy with editing rather than a separate step. The downside is time: drafting copy takes longer than generating filler, so use it once the layout is mostly settled.

Structured placeholders

Sometimes the clearest placeholder is not a paragraph at all but a labelled box: 'Product description, 40 to 60 words' or 'Customer quote goes here'. Structured placeholders tell everyone exactly what content is needed, how long it should be, and who is responsible for it. They are especially useful in content-heavy projects with many contributors, where a paragraph of Latin says nothing about what should replace it.

Choosing the right alternative

Match the filler to the audience and the stage. Use classic lorem ipsum for early layout work, English filler for clients who dislike Latin, themed generators for internal fun, and real draft copy once the design is close to final. Whatever you choose, keep it consistent within a project, label it as a placeholder, and remove it before launch. The filler is a tool, not a feature.

AI-generated filler text

A newer alternative is filler text written by an AI tool, which can produce realistic-sounding paragraphs on any topic in seconds. This can be useful, because the text reads like real copy and tests the design with realistic sentence structures. The risk is that AI filler can sound plausible but say nothing, or invent facts that a reader might believe. Treat AI filler like any other placeholder: label it clearly, never publish it, and replace it with real, checked copy before launch.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best lorem ipsum alternative?

For most work, plain English filler or early draft copy. Classic lorem ipsum is still best for pure layout testing.

Are funny lorem ipsum generators professional?

For internal mockups, yes. For client-facing work, stick to the classic text or neutral English filler.

Can I write my own filler text?

Yes. Short, neutral sentences that mimic real copy lengths work well, and they avoid the Latin that some clients find confusing.

Is AI-generated filler better than lorem ipsum?

It reads more naturally and tests realistic sentence structure, but it can invent plausible-sounding facts, so label it and replace it before launch.
